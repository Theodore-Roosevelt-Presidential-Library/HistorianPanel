# House style check

Read `reference/panel-rules.md` at the plugin root first. This skill checks
wording, not facts; facts go through `accuracy-review`. The table records the
Library's house wording, not a historical finding; what happened where goes to
`sp-navy-rough-riders`.

## What it checks

The Library's Story Guide governs tone and terminology. This skill checks the
rules most often broken, and the ones that travel with factual errors. If the
person has the Story Guide or the Brand and Editorial Standards to hand, those
win over this list.

| Check | Use | Instead of |
|-------|-----|------------|
| The Rough Riders' charge | "Kettle Hill"; the wider battle is "the San Juan Heights" | "San Juan Hill" in prose (allowed only inside a proper name or book title) |
| North Dakota landforms | buttes | mesas |
| His name | "T.R." with periods; "Theodore Roosevelt" | "Teddy" (a hashtag is the only exception) |
| People who visit | participants | visitors, guests |
| Governing board | Trustees | board members |
| People who give | Benefactors | donors |
| Institution names | "the Library," "the Foundation," capitalized | lowercase "library" for this institution |
| Sustainability | "pursuing" certification | "certified," "achieved," until confirmed |
| Funding | "independently operated" | "privately funded" |
| Filler | specific detail | "world-class experience," "transformative journey," unverifiable superlatives |
| Throat-clearing | lead with the fact | "it is worth noting," "it bears mentioning," "to put it plainly" |

## Procedure

1. Read the whole piece once for voice: friendly, plain, specific, never
   stuffy, and politically even-handed.
2. Go through the table above line by line and list every hit with its
   location.
3. Mark anything that is also a factual claim (for example "San Juan Hill"
   attached to a date or a unit) so the coordinator sends it to the right
   specialist.
4. Suggest replacement wording for each hit, explaining why in one short line.

## Output

A table: location, current wording, suggested wording, rule. Then one line
with the count. Do not rewrite the whole piece unless asked.
