# Conservation and public lands

Read `reference/panel-rules.md` at the plugin root first.

## Scope

- Designations made or signed during T.R.'s presidency: forest reserves and
  national forests, national monuments, federal bird reservations, game
  preserves, national parks, reclamation projects.
- Totals: acreage and counts, and what each total includes.
- Dates of designations and the instrument used (proclamation, executive order,
  act of Congress).
- Conservation speeches and conferences (wording goes to `sp-quote-verifier`).

## Out of scope

- Quote wording → `sp-quote-verifier`
- Rankings against other presidents → `sp-superlatives` (also routed here)
- Present-day status of a site → the managing agency's current page; flag if it
  matters

## Where to look first

1. **The instrument itself:** the proclamation or executive order text (American
   Presidency Project; agency compilations; *U.S. Statutes at Large* for acts
   and for proclamations printed there).
2. **Managing agencies:** National Park Service (including "Theodore Roosevelt
   and Conservation" and the NPS list of national monuments by designation
   date); USDA Forest Service history publications; U.S. Fish and Wildlife
   Service history of the National Wildlife Refuge System.
3. **Forest History Society** for forest reserve and national forest history.
4. **T.R.'s own account** in *An Autobiography*, which gives his counts. Cite it
   as his claim and check it against the agency.
5. **Conference proceedings:** the 1905 American Forest Congress and the 1908
   Conference of Governors, for what was said and when.

## Known traps

- Counts differ by what is counted. Say which category and whose count, and use
  the figure the sources agree on. [ref: D-03]
- Acreage totals mix categories (forests, monuments, refuges). Say what the
  total includes. [ref: D-13]
- Rankings against later presidents fail. Rank only what a source ranks.
  [ref: D-13, D-14]
- Conservation quotes are truncated without ellipses. [ref: H-04, H-05, H-08]
- Credit for a designation made by Congress is given to T.R. alone, or the
  reverse. Name the instrument. [method]
- A site's later name or status (a monument later made a park) is used for the
  date of the original designation. [method]
- The Library's Public Lands Map and similar tools are not independent sources.
  [house-rule]

## Procedure

1. Name the site or the category, the date, and the instrument claimed.
2. Find the instrument's text, then an agency history page.
3. For totals, find two sources that count the same thing, and state the
   category.
4. Show any arithmetic.
5. Write the narrowest accurate correction.
