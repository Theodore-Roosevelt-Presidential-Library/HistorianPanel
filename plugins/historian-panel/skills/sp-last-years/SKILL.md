# River of Doubt, World War I and death

Read `reference/panel-rules.md` at the plugin root first.

## Scope

- *An Autobiography*: its serial and book publication and its editions.
- The South American trip and the Roosevelt-Rondon Scientific Expedition:
  members, route, dates on the river, the illness, the naming of the river,
  the return and the dispute over the findings.
- His writing and speaking on preparedness and the war, his relations
  with the Wilson administration, and the volunteer-division proposal.
- The family at war as it bears on T.R.: the sons' enlistments and units in
  outline, Quentin's death and how and when the news reached the family.
- The 1916 and 1918 political seasons as they touch him.
- His last illness, death, funeral and burial.

## Out of scope

- Ranks, units, decorations and later careers of Ted Jr., Kermit, Archie and
  Quentin → `sp-family-offices`
- Exact wording of telegrams, letters and remarks about Quentin, and of lines
  attributed to others at T.R.'s death → `sp-quote-verifier`
- Military and naval detail of the war → `sp-military-naval` (planned; flag as
  a roster gap until active)
- Rivers, place names and maps in Brazil and France → `sp-maps-places`
  (planned) and `sp-chronology-calendar` for names that changed
- Indigenous peoples met on the expedition → the Library's human reviewers
- Photographs and film → `sp-photo-id`

## Where to look first

1. **T.R.'s own books:** *Through the Brazilian Wilderness* (1914; Project
   Gutenberg https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11746) for the expedition's dated
   narrative and appendices, and *An Autobiography* (1913;
   https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/3335). Note which edition you read.
2. **Library of Congress Theodore Roosevelt Papers**
   (https://www.loc.gov/collections/theodore-roosevelt-papers/) and the
   Theodore Roosevelt Center digital library
   (https://www.theodorerooseveltcenter.org/digital-library/) for wartime
   letters, telegrams and drafts.
3. **National Park Service, Sagamore Hill National Historic Site**
   (https://www.nps.gov/sahi/index.htm) and its history pages
   (https://www.nps.gov/sahi/learn/historyculture/index.htm) for the last
   years at Oyster Bay, his death and burial.
4. **American Battle Monuments Commission** (https://www.abmc.gov/) for the
   burial records of American war dead in Europe; and the National Archives
   (https://www.archives.gov/research) for service records.
5. **Period newspapers** in page images through Chronicling America
   (https://chroniclingamerica.loc.gov/). Wire reports of Quentin's death and
   of T.R.'s death carry dispatch dates that differ from issue dates.
6. **Brazilian sources:** reports of the Rondon Commission and Brazilian
   government records for the river's naming; name the record you used.
7. **Secondary, for leads only:** Candice Millard, *The River of Doubt* (2005);
   Edmund Morris, *Colonel Roosevelt* (2010); Patricia O'Toole, *When Trumpets
   Call* (2005). Follow their notes to the primary record.

## Known traps

- The river T.R. descended is confused with a larger river, or with the Amazon
  itself, and its length or the extent of its renaming is overstated. Check
  what was renamed, by whom and when, in the expedition's own account and in
  Brazilian records. [method]
- Expedition dates are taken from the start of the South American trip, the
  start of the river descent or the arrival at a named place without saying
  which. Use the dated entries in *Through the Brazilian Wilderness*. [method]
- The sons' wartime service is misstated: the wrong army, the wrong unit, a
  command they did not hold, or later honors moved into the war years. Hand
  the details to `sp-family-offices` and check each against service records.
  [ref: A-03, A-18, C-19]
- The place where Quentin fell, where he was first buried and where he lies now
  are merged, and French place names are given in the wrong form. Check the
  ABMC record and period reports, and keep the three places separate. [method]
- Telegrams and letters about Quentin are quoted with wording that does not
  match the printed text, or credited to the wrong sender or date. Route to
  `sp-quote-verifier` and find the dispatch. [method]
- Lines said about T.R. at his death are presented as direct quotation when
  they come from a later memoir. Label them as recalled. [method]
- Claims that grief over Quentin caused his death are stated as fact. Give
  what the medical and family records say and keep interpretation out of the
  verdict. [method]
- The date he died, the date the news was printed and the date of the funeral
  are confused. Check each. [method]
- The Library's own timelines and datasets are not independent sources.
  [house-rule]

## Procedure

1. Pin the claim to a date, a place and the milestone it describes (left,
   reached, died, buried, published, announced).
2. For the expedition, read T.R.'s dated narrative and check names and
   lengths against Brazilian records.
3. For the war years, use dated letters and telegrams, then period newspapers
   read in page images, noting dispatch date and issue date.
4. Hand sons' service details, quotations and images to their specialists.
5. Check every place name for period and modern forms.
6. Compute any age, interval or count and show the arithmetic.
7. Write the narrowest accurate correction.
