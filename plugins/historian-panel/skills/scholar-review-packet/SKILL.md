# Scholar review packet

Read `reference/panel-rules.md` at the plugin root first.

The panel narrows the work; a real scholar makes the call. This packet respects
the reviewer's time: they should spend it on judgment, not on looking things up.

## Before building

1. The piece must have been through `accuracy-review` (and ideally
   `review-lenses`). If not, run them first.
2. Ask who the reviewer is (role is enough: the curator, an outside scholar, a
   Trustee) and whether the packet stays inside the Library or goes outside.

## What goes in

1. **One-paragraph brief:** what the piece is, where it will appear, the
   audience, and the deadline.
2. **What we need from you:** at most five specific questions, ranked. For
   example, "Is 'often described as the only soldier...' acceptable, or should
   we cut it?" Never "please review."
3. **Open items first:** unresolved claims, curator questions, lens findings
   two or more lenses flagged, and every claim the panel corrected. For each:
   current text, the panel's proposed text, the sources with links, and why.
4. **Closed claims** in a compact table at the end: claim, verdict, two sources.
   The reviewer can skim or skip it.
5. **The piece itself**, with open items marked in the text.

## Rules

- **Going outside the Library:** paraphrase only from licensed works and link
  only to public sources. No licensed text, no internal file paths, no ledger
  IDs, no other open items unrelated to the piece.
- Never claim the reviewer has approved anything. Their reply is recorded in
  the ledger as their decision, with date and role.
- Do not send the packet. Prepare it and let the person send it; sending a
  message on someone's behalf needs their approval.

## Output

A document (the Docs type when available, otherwise Markdown) titled
"Review packet: <piece>," plus one line telling the person what the reviewer
is being asked to decide.
